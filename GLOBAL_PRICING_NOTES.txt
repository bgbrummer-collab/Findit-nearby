FindIt Global Pricing
=====================
1. Exact retailer-feed price and currency are always shown as the primary price.
2. Browser locale chooses a preferred display currency without requesting precise location.
3. If the current retailer feed is ZAR and the visitor uses another supported currency,
   FindIt may show a clearly-labelled approximate converted amount underneath.
4. Converted prices are NEVER labelled as retailer prices.
5. When country-specific retailer feeds are added later, FindIt should prioritize those
   exact local feeds instead of currency conversion.
6. SmartBuyGlasses ZA remains a South African feed; its source-of-truth price is ZAR.
