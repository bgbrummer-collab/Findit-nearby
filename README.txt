FindIt Nearby V4

V4 replaces the fake sample stores with real OpenStreetMap/Overpass nearby businesses and a Leaflet map.

It includes:
- camera/photo upload
- browser location
- real nearby mapped businesses
- distance calculation
- map
- directions

Important: V4 does NOT claim an exact product is in stock. Exact visual AI + retailer product/stock matching is the next stage.
